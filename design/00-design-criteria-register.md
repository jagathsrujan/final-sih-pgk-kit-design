# DRISHTI-PGK — Design Criteria Register

**Problem statement:** SIH26098 — *Development of a Low-Cost Precision Guidance and Smart Electronic Fuze System for a 155 mm Artillery Shell*
**Organisation:** Ministry of Defence (MoD) / Yantra India Limited, Ambajhari, Nagpur
**Purpose of this file:** the single working record of every criterion the design must satisfy or decide. It is the evidence base for the submission — every number that appears in the submission should trace back to a row here.

---

## How to use this file

**Every criterion carries one of three types:**

| Type | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Given** | Imposed by the customer, the platform, or a defence standard | Extract it. Do not invent it. |
| **Derived** | Falls out of physics once the givens are fixed | Compute it. Show the arithmetic and the assumptions. |
| **Chosen** | A design decision of ours | Decide it, and justify it against the givens with a trade. |

**Status values — keep them honest:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `OPEN` | Not yet defined |
| `OPEN-BLOCKED` | *(retired 2026-09-17 — superseded by `PARAMETRIC`)* Cannot be fixed until external data arrives. |
| `PARAMETRIC` | **Sized over a declared envelope built from public data** (`design/02-reference-155mm-model.md`). Neither blocked nor parked: a bounded design value is in use, with a stated public basis and a sensitivity showing how the design closes across the envelope. Every PARAMETRIC value is re-baselined at P6. |
| `PROPOSED` | A value is proposed but not yet confirmed by the team |
| `DEFINED` | Confirmed by the team |
| `DERIVED` | Computed from other defined criteria |
| `VERIFIED` | Confirmed by analysis, simulation or test |

**Governing criteria are marked ⚑.** These are where the margins are thinnest and where the design will be won or lost. Get these right before anything else.

**Values marked *provisional* are derived on top of a placeholder, an unconfirmed anchor, or a [ref] value.** A provisional value names the input it depends on and never counts as closed, however solid the arithmetic looks.

**Values marked **[ref]** are carried from the reference literature for the 155 mm class (`notes/00-PGK-thesis-working-notes.md`) and must be confirmed against YIL's actual shell.**

---

## The dependency chain

Criteria cannot be defined out of order. This is the sequence:

```
M  Mission (target, range, charge)
     └─ baseline unguided dispersion
         └─ required correction capability
             └─ G  guidance & control requirements
                 └─ C  canard sizing → Taero → alternator sizing → friction budget
                     └─ N  navigation & sensing requirements
                         └─ W  power, thermal
                             └─ S/P/E/F  structure, stability, environment, fuze
                                 └─ K/R  cost, verification
```

S  (Stability) and P (Platform) run in parallel with the chain — they constrain it, and they are gated on the YIL shell data.

| G1 | Guidance law type (impact-point prediction / trajectory shaping) | Chosen | **Continuous impact-point prediction (IPP), with the apogee no-command blackout and a systematic-bias trim term folded in as provisions of the law itself.** Each cycle: propagate the nav state forward to impact, compare the predicted impact point with the target, and command a ring angle that drives the predicted miss to zero. Uses the whole usable window and spends authority in proportion to the removable miss. | G2 | Guidance law output (ring-angle command) | Chosen | **A commanded earth-referenced ring angle θ_gk, emitted as a position reference to the outer position loop (option A). The magnitude is not commanded.** This matches what the hardware can accept: steering comes from cant angle, not deflection, so the guidance force magnitude is set by cant angle and dynamic pressure and rotating the ring only *aims* it — there are no moving surfaces and the cant is fixed at manufacture. **The ring angle is therefore the sole command channel.** Three consequences: (1) "not correcting" is impossible — the force is on from launch to impact, and the law can only null the *net* impulse by time-sharing the aim direction, so M7's authority budget is unaffected but the instantaneous perturbation never vanishes; (2) the IPP propagation model **must include the always-on steering force**, or G4's 13 m propagation term is wrong; (3) the aero model stays out of the control loop, so risk 8 does not reach the controller. Guidance update rate: **≤ 10 Hz ceiling, ~6 Hz design point** — this was recorded as a 10 Hz *floor* and corrected at G10, which showed that 10 Hz is exactly the outer loop's settling time (0.103 s) and therefore a ceiling, not a floor. The command is smooth and the outer loop bandwidth is 6.2 Hz | Method choice: user 2026-09-17 (option A). Keeps the retained cascade interface exactly as validated (P outer position loop at 6.2 Hz). The RSS may understate the true 1σ until the two are separated when R5's input distributions are defined | G4, R5, risk 8 | Open |
| 17 | G3, G14 and G15 are now one mechanism driven by a single estimate — the DOB's disturbance current — so a defect in the observer is a defect in all three simultaneously: the outer authority gate, the dynamic current limit and the disturbance compensation would all be wrong in the same direction, and none of them would reveal it. **G12 extends the coupling**: the clamping limit is the same moving quantity, so a wrong estimate also freezes the integrator against the wrong threshold, and the failure surfaces as the runaway that G11 forbids rather than as a disturbance error | G3, G11, G12, G14, G15, R7 | Open |
| 18 | With fixed-incidence canards the guidance force magnitude cannot be commanded — the ring angle only aims it — so the force is on from launch to impact and "not correcting" is impossible. The IPP propagation model must therefore include the always-on steering force or G4's 13 m propagation term is wrong, and the trajectory is continuously perturbed whether or not a correction is wanted. Time-sharing the aim direction nulls the net impulse but never the instantaneous perturbation | G1, G2, G4, C2, C8, risk 8 | Open |
| 19 | The G10 consequence gives the flight processor a concrete load — roughly 300 IPP propagations per flight, each an integration to impact — and **no criterion in the register owns the flight computer** (throughput, memory, timing margin). **G13 states the requirement and removes the control-timing risk**: the control loop sits in FPGA fabric so its 1 ms timing is independent of the load, and the processor's deadline is one propagation per ~0.17 s guidance interval. What remains open is that the requirement has no criterion of its own — it lives in G13 and here. The reference work's own slip-ring current mismatch, 5 A rated against 8 A drawn, is the precedent for how this kind of gap reaches hardware | G1, G10, G13, R3, R7 | Open — reduced, requirement now stated |
| 20 | **The design rests on a reference shell, not YIL's — the largest single assumption in the project.** Every P- and S-domain criterion is now sized against a public-data model of the M795 / M549A1 class, and the kit is specified as **limits it satisfies across a declared envelope** rather than values inherited from one projectile. If YIL's shell sits outside that envelope — mass, inertia pair, ogive contour or fuze-well depth — both the sizing chain and the kit envelope move, and the Sg result moves first. **Mitigation: the P6 re-baseline, which is that phase's entire purpose, plus declaring the envelope openly in the submission so nothing rests on an implied claim of YIL data.** The kit's parametric specification is what makes this a defined product rather than a guess | P1–P14, S1–S7, M3, M4, M6, C4, C5, C26, C31 | Open — accepted, mitigated, and declared |
| 21 | **The platform inertia pair carried in the register as `[ref]` diverges from the public record, in the direction that flatters the design.** Ixx 0.144 agrees within 5 %; **Iyy 1.216 sits 28 % below the public-calibrated band of 1.66–1.81 kg·m²**, and since **Sg ∝ Ixx²/Iyy** a low Iyy makes the stability result look better than it is. The register now uses the conservative band and states the divergence rather than inheriting the flattering value. Residual exposure: the band is calibrated on a single published mass-property set with class correction factors (kx 1.401, ky 0.929), so it carries model uncertainty as well as platform uncertainty | P4, P5, S1, S2, S3, M7 | Open — conservative value adopted, model uncertainty stated |
