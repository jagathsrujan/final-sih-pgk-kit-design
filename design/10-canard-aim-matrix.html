Reading the figure

Blue = steering pair (fixed at 0°/180° on the ring, equal cant 10°): both fins produce forces in the same direction → the thin arrows are parallel and add, the roll torques cancel. Net force 2·F·sinδ ≥ 18.1 N at the design point (C8).

Amber = control pair (fixed at 90°/270°, opposite cant 10°): forces oppose → zero net force, a pure roll couple T_aero = 2·F·sinδ·r about −X. The couple is identical in all four panels — steering never disturbs the torque balance.

The bold arrow is the net guidance force and always acts perpendicular to the steering pair’s 0–180 line, i.e. along the control pair’s line: push direction = steering-line ± 90°. The four ring angles are 90° apart; opposite directions are 180° apart. The dashed arc is θ_gk drawn the shortest way round, which is how the controller rotates the ring (G11/G12).

IN FLIGHT — the shell does not swerve where the force points (G16). A control force ahead of the CG swerves a spinning round at a phase off the force direction: φ_d ≈ 172° at launch → 146–149° near apogee → 163° at 85 s [C11]. The command is θ_gk = ψ_swerve − φ_d − 90°: to move LEFT with φ_d = 158° the ring sits at θ_gk ≈ 202° — the force is aimed about 158° away from left. φ_d for this airframe is OPEN (G16, a P3 item).

Magnitude is never commanded (G2). The force is on from launch to impact; a partial correction is a dwell-fraction phasor — hold the aim azimuth for d = a_cmd / a_auth(t) and park at aim ±90° (zero mean) for the rest of the cycle.
